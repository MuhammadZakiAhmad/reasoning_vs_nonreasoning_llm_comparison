**Open questions / blockers**:
- **The IFT model does not look non-reasoning.** Its chickens/cows reply was a full worked solution — define variables, two equations, solve by substitution, markdown + LaTeX — not a direct answer. That undercuts the project's central contrast. Either the "ift" checkpoint also emits reasoning-style text, or the prompts themselves (multi-step math) force step-by-step output from both. Test with `prompts[0]` ("capital of France") to find out. Resolve this before building on the comparison.
- `MAX_NEW_TOKENS = 256` truncates. The IFT answer was cut mid-equation at `x = 10 - y = 1`. Both models write long-form, so 256 captures a different fraction of each output, which affects what the attention average means.
- VRAM headroom is thin: two float32 1.5B models ≈ 12.4 GB against 14.56 GB. Reasoning generation attention collection is the untested peak. If it OOMs, next steps are float16 (T4 has no bf16) or processing one model at a time and freeing between.
