**Lessons learned**:
- **Verify the mechanism, not just the symptom.** The eos bug was found by printing decoded tokens around the cut, and confirmed by printing the model's own configured eos. One line of diagnostic output ended two sessions of wrong theory. Same move now available for free: print the window each model's average covers, side by side, before interpreting any comparison.

Checking whether the prediction is *exhausted* by the artifact before crediting a model difference is the cheap order of operations.
